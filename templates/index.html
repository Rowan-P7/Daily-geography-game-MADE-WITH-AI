A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

How to play

Every day brings seven rounds drawn from eight geography mini-games. Everyone gets the same UTC-dated challenge, with a different mix of mechanics and difficulty.

Scoring: Rounds are worth up to 1,000 base points, with speed bonuses where appropriate. Solve mystery countries with fewer clues, get closer with estimates, and find more correct flags or neighbours for partial credit. Consecutive correct rounds add a combo bonus.

Your daily attempt: You can pause and come back, but you only get one daily run. Practice is unlimited and never changes your streak or stats.

Keyboard: Use Tab to move between controls and Enter to submit or continue. Use 1–4 for any conventional multiple-choice questions.
